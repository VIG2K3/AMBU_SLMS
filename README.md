About AMBU SLMS:

Ambu is a manufacturer of medical equipment. To maintain the quality of its products, this company primarily relies on accurate and traceable Shelf-Life Management. Previously, Ambu's system involved creating barcodes manually, reminding the product owner to test the goods via email, and manually entering product details into Excel to stay on top of things. This technique frequently results in human mistakes, misunderstandings, and delays in receiving email reminders.
